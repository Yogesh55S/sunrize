Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Sunrise Transit Limited respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you give us through this website.

What we collect

Your name and contact details, your collection and delivery addresses, the details of your move, and any photos or notes you send us through our quote or review forms.

How we use it

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and to arrange the service you ask for. We do not sell your data.

Reviews

If you submit a review, your first name, your rating and your comments may be shown on our website.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it to provide the service and to meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. To do so, email info@sunrisetransit.co.uk.
